# Masteko Website Recommendations

## Implemented in the Oxford-inspired version

- A restrained, institutional visual system built around large typography, white space, real photography and a limited forest/lime palette.
- Clear separation of owner representation, property operations, accounting/reporting and systems.
- Concrete operating proof: the $75M Campus Habitations platform, 30+ entities and partnerships, bilingual delivery and named representative mandates.
- An investor-facing operating-platform section that explains the path from source accounting to the quarterly owner package.
- Governance language covering decision authority, traceability, risk exceptions and accountability.
- A management-team section with real photography for Marc Koran, Natali Slutsker, Steve Lombardi, Peter Taylor, Thiha Nyi Nyi and Nicole Koran.
- Direct access to the existing investor portal.
- Full English/French page copy, responsive navigation, keyboard focus states and reduced-motion support.

## Recommended next improvements

### 1. Replace good source photography with a Masteko-owned image library

Commission one consistent half-day shoot covering the six team members, one working session, two building inspections and three representative assets. Consistent framing and colour treatment will materially improve the team grid and eliminate dependence on photos created for other platforms.

### 2. Add three proper mandate pages

Create dedicated pages for Campus Habitations, Bukoval and TGLP/MBH. Each page should show the mandate, asset type, Masteko scope, control structure, reporting cadence and outcomes that can be disclosed. Avoid publishing rent, valuation or investor information unless specifically approved.

### 3. Publish a sample quarterly reporting package

Offer a carefully redacted Bukoval or MBH quarterly report as a downloadable PDF. This is stronger proof for an institutional investor than general service descriptions. Include a clear “sample / illustrative” label and remove confidential tenant, lender and ownership information.

### 4. Add an acquisitions and sale-leaseback capability page

Explain how Masteko supports sourcing, underwriting, tenant-credit review, LOI negotiation, due diligence, financing, closing and post-closing asset management. This page should be written for funds such as Alderan without naming a confidential opportunity.

### 5. Add reporting dates to every metric

Institutional sites date their facts. Confirm the reporting date and definition for the $75M platform and 30+ entities, then disclose “as at [date]” in the page footer or beside the metrics.

### 6. Strengthen governance and compliance pages

Before a broader launch, add privacy, accessibility, conflicts, complaint/contact and responsible-investing pages appropriate to Masteko’s actual legal and regulatory obligations. Have counsel confirm the final wording.

### 7. Build a restrained research section

Publish only substantive notes: Québec and Canadian cap rates, net-lease structures, sale-leaseback underwriting, covenant monitoring and quarterly operating observations. A small number of specific articles will improve credibility and search visibility more than frequent generic posts.

### 8. Complete bilingual SEO

Move the French version to a crawlable `/fr/` URL when the content stabilizes, with translated page titles, descriptions, canonical tags and `hreflang` references. The current toggle is good for review but separate URLs are better for search engines.

### 9. Add measurement without compromising privacy

Measure mandate inquiries, investor-login clicks, report downloads and case-study engagement. Avoid invasive tracking and publish a clear consent approach if analytics cookies are introduced.

### 10. Establish content ownership

Assign one person to verify metrics and mandates quarterly, one person to approve investor-facing financial language and one person to publish changes. Maintain a short source register for every public claim.
